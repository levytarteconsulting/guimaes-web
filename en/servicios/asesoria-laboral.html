What our labor advisory includes

Labor management is the part of a company you notice most when it fails: a miscalculated payslip, the wrong type of contract or a sick leave not reported on time end up in claims, surcharges or a bad atmosphere nobody needs. Our job is to make all of that work every month without you having to think about it.

  • Payroll and social security. We calculate payroll for your whole workforce and prepare social security contributions and official filings on time, month after month.
  • Hiring. We help you choose the right type of contract for each role, check any incentives that may apply and process registrations, terminations and changes.
  • Day-to-day events. Sick leave, holidays, permits, changes to working hours, leaves of absence: we process them and reflect them in payroll.
  • Dismissals and terminations. We calculate severance and final settlements, prepare the paperwork, assess the risk before you act and support you at conciliation if it comes to that.
  • Labor inspections. We prepare the documentation requested, accompany you at the inspection and defend your position.
  • Ongoing labor advice. The applicable collective agreement, shift organisation, pay policy or how to approach a difficult conversation with an employee: questions that come up any day and deserve a clear answer.

Which companies we work with

We work with companies about to hire their first employee, with SMEs that already have a stable workforce and want to outsource labor management with full assurance, and with growing companies that bring people on board every few months and need the admin side not to slow the business down. If you are about to take that first step, we recommend reading our guide to hiring your first employee.

We also support companies going through a delicate moment: a dismissal that has to be done properly, a workforce restructuring or an inspection that has just arrived. In those cases, what adds most value is having someone who already knows your company and does not have to start from scratch.

How we work

We start by reviewing your company's labor situation: contracts in force, applicable collective agreement, calendar, recent payroll and any open issues. With that we tell you what is fine, what should be corrected and in what order.

From then on, each month you send us the changes (new hires, leavers, hours, variable pay) and we take care of the rest: payroll, social security, filings and records. You have a reference contact who knows your team and whom you can call whenever a question comes up, with no switchboards or tickets.

Why Guimaes

We have been advising companies for 40 years and more than 100 rely on us on an ongoing basis. The difference compared with a payroll bureau is that labor matters do not run on their own: they are connected to accounting, to tax and to the legal and compliance side. When you decide how to pay a manager, whether to hire or outsource, or how to handle a dismissal, you see every consequence, not just the labor one.

What we aim for is that people management stops being a source of risk and becomes orderly and predictable. That is why we work with no lock-in and with clear fees from the start.

FAQ

Questions about Labor & Payroll

Do you handle all payroll every month?+

Yes. We calculate and issue payroll, social security and official filings on time every month. You just tell us about any changes.

I'm hiring my first employee, what do I need?+

We handle everything: choosing the type of contract, checking any incentives that may apply, social security registration and reporting the contract.

Will you support me in a dismissal?+

Yes. We assess the risk before you act, calculate severance and the final settlement, prepare the paperwork and support you at conciliation if needed.

What if I get a labor inspection?+

We assist you throughout: we prepare the documentation requested, accompany you before the inspector and defend your interests.

Do you also manage sick leave and holidays?+

Yes. We process sick leave, keep track of holidays and permits and keep every workforce change up to date.

I already have an adviser, is it hard to switch?+

No. We request the documentation from your current adviser, review contracts and recent payroll and handle the handover in an orderly way so that no month is left uncovered.
