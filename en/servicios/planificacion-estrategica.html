What our strategic planning services include

Every company has a strategy, even if it isn't written down. The difference is whether that strategy rests on numbers and scenarios or on intuition. Our strategic planning advice turns your goals into a concrete plan, with figures, assumptions and alternatives, that helps you decide today and explain your decisions tomorrow to partners, banks or investors.

  • Business plan and financial model. Multi-year projections of revenue, costs, investment and cash, with explicit assumptions that can be reviewed and discussed.
  • Scenarios. What happens if sales grow less than expected, a key cost rises or you open a new market. Seeing several scenarios before committing greatly reduces the risk of a decision.
  • Company valuation. Using different methods depending on the purpose: partners joining or leaving, a sale or purchase, or negotiation with investors.
  • Corporate transactions. Structuring acquisitions, sales, mergers and reorganisations, and preparing or reviewing due diligence.
  • Funding rounds. Model, valuation, investor documentation and support in negotiating and in the legal and tax structuring of the investor's entry.

Which companies we work with

Our strategic planning services are a particularly good fit for companies at a turning point: those about to scale up, open a new business line or enter another market; startups preparing a round that need a credible plan for investors; and partners facing a transaction —buying another company, selling their own or bringing in a new partner— who want to structure it properly. We also work with family businesses preparing for succession that need to put their strategy in order before handing it over.

How we work

We start by listening: what you want to achieve, in what timeframe and with what resources. Then we analyse your starting point using the company's real data —accounts, margins, cash, structure— and build the model together with you, so you understand every assumption and can defend it.

The plan doesn't go in a drawer. We review it regularly against reality, adjust what needs adjusting and, when an important decision comes up, analyse it on the same model. If you need someone to handle that follow-up on an ongoing basis, we combine it with our outsourced CFO service.

Why Guimaes

With 40 years of experience and more than 100 companies advised on an ongoing basis, we know that a good strategy often fails in execution: because of a tax cost nobody foresaw, a badly resolved partners' agreement or cash that can't sustain the plan. FAQ

Questions about Strategic Planning

What exactly is a business plan for?+

To put numbers and scenarios to your strategy: it helps you decide, convince investors or banks and anticipate cash needs.

Can you value my company?+

Yes. We carry out valuations using different methods depending on the purpose: new partners, a sale or purchase, or negotiation with investors.

What is due diligence and when do I need it?+

It is an in-depth review of a company before a transaction. We prepare it if you are selling or review the target's if you are buying.

Do you support the whole funding round?+

Yes. From the model and valuation to negotiating the term sheet and the legal and tax structuring of the investor's entry.

How often is the strategic plan reviewed?+

We review it regularly against actual results and, in any case, whenever a significant decision comes up or the starting assumptions change.

Do you work in coordination with tax and legal?+

Always. The advantage of GUIMAES is that strategy, tax and legal are decided together, with no silos or contradictory versions.
