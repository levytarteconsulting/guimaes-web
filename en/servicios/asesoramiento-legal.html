What our legal advisory includes

Most of a company's legal problems don't start in court: they start with a contract signed in a hurry, articles of association nobody reviewed or an agreement between partners that was never put in writing. Our legal advisory is designed so those problems never arise.

  • Commercial and corporate law. Company formation, articles of association, general meetings, capital increases and reductions, changes of directors and restructuring transactions.
  • Contracts. Drafting and reviewing contracts with customers, suppliers, distributors and partners, leases and confidentiality agreements, flagging clauses that could harm you before you sign.
  • Shareholder agreements. The document that governs entries and exits, decision-making, deadlocks and disputes. If you have partners, it is one of the first things to get right; we explain it in this article on shareholder agreements.
  • Regulatory compliance. Review of the obligations that apply to your business and the internal protocols to meet them, with an approach proportionate to the size of your company.
  • Data protection. Compliance with the GDPR and Spanish regulations: record of processing activities, processor agreements, website legal notices and internal procedures.

Which companies we work with

We support entrepreneurs about to incorporate who want to get it right from the start, SMEs signing significant contracts with customers or suppliers, partners joining or leaving a company and groups reorganising their corporate structure. Also companies that handle personal data of customers or employees and need to comply without turning data protection into an obstacle for the business.

How we work

First we understand your business and the context of each matter: reviewing a contract with your biggest customer is not the same as one with an occasional supplier. We explain the risks in plain language, propose alternatives and, if there is something to negotiate, help you do it.

As well as one-off matters, we offer ongoing support so you can raise any day-to-day legal question without opening a new file each time. And when a dispute arises with a customer or supplier, we first look for a negotiated way out that protects both the relationship and your interests.

FAQ

Questions about Legal & Compliance

Do you review contracts before signing?+

Yes. We analyse and draft commercial and service contracts, leases and agreements, and warn you about clauses that could harm you.

What is a shareholder agreement and do I need one?+

It is the document that governs the relationship between partners: entries, exits, decisions and disputes. If you have partners, it saves you very costly problems.

Do you help set up a company?+

Yes. We advise you on the most suitable legal form and handle the incorporation, articles of association and registry formalities.

Do you cover data protection compliance?+

Yes. We bring your company into line with the GDPR and Spanish data protection law: records of processing, processor agreements, legal notices and protocols.

What if a dispute arises with a customer or supplier?+

First we look for a negotiated solution that protects the relationship and your interests. If that is not possible, we advise you on the right course of action.

Can I ask you occasional legal questions?+

Yes. As well as specific matters, we offer ongoing support so you can resolve day-to-day legal questions with a contact who already knows your company.
