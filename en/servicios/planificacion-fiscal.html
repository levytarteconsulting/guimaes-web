What our tax advisory includes

Filing taxes is compulsory; planning them is what makes the difference. A company that only looks at its taxes when a deadline is due is late for almost every decision that could have reduced its tax bill legally. Our work combines both: complying without errors and planning ahead.

  • Corporate Tax. Calculation and filing of the annual return and instalment payments, reviewing the adjustments, deductions and incentives that apply to your company.
  • VAT and periodic returns. Quarterly or monthly returns, withholdings, intra-EU transactions and informative returns, always on time.
  • Planning before year-end. Before the financial year ends we estimate the result and review which decisions —investments, depreciation, reserves, pay for partners and directors— are worth taking while there is still room to act.
  • International tax. Cross-border transactions, double taxation treaties and structuring of international activities.
  • Inspections and requests. We handle requests and verification and inspection procedures from start to finish, and represent you before the tax authorities.
  • Adapting to e-invoicing. We get you ready for Verifactu and mandatory e-invoicing, according to the timetable that applies to you.

Tax planning for companies: who it is for

Tax planning is not only for large groups. A growing SME, a company with recurring profits or a business owner wondering how to draw an income from their company have as much to gain as a multinational, and often more, because each decision weighs more heavily on their result. We work with companies that want to comply without scares, with businesses that operate outside Spain and with groups and holdings that need a coherent tax structure across their companies.

If you want an idea of where to start, on our blog we explain how to plan an SME's taxes and which deductible expenses are most often overlooked.

How we work

We start with a diagnosis of your tax position: returns for recent years, corporate structure, usual operations and open risks. With that we draw up your company's own tax calendar, with its deadlines and decision points.

During the year we review each quarter before filing, not afterwards. In the last quarter we carry out specific year-end planning, which is when decisions that affect the tax can still be taken. And if a request arrives, we already know your company and your numbers, which makes the response much faster and more solid.

FAQ

Questions about Tax Planning

Is tax optimisation legal?+

Yes. Planning means correctly applying the rules, deductions and incentives that are yours. We never propose schemes outside the law.

When should I start planning the year-end?+

Ideally after closing the third quarter, when the year's result is fairly well known and there is still time to make decisions.

What is Verifactu and does it affect me?+

It is the Tax Agency's verifiable invoicing system. It affects most companies and sole traders according to a phased timetable; we help you adapt before it becomes mandatory for you.

Will you support me if I get a tax inspection?+

Yes. We handle requests and inspections from start to finish, prepare the documentation and represent you before the authorities.

I operate in several countries, can you help?+

Yes. We work on international tax: double taxation treaties, intra-EU VAT and structuring of cross-border operations.

How often do you review my tax position?+

Continuously throughout the year, with milestones at each quarterly close and specific planning before year-end.
