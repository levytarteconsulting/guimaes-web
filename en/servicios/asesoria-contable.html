What our accounting service includes

Well-kept accounts are good for much more than meeting your obligations to the Tax Agency and the Companies Register. They are the source of the data you use to decide whether to hire, invest, borrow or raise prices. That is why we don't just record invoices: we close every month and tell you what the numbers say.

  • Financial accounting. Recording of income, expenses, assets and financing in line with the Spanish chart of accounts, with consistent criteria from one year to the next.
  • Monthly closes. Bank reconciliation, review of customer and supplier balances and period adjustments, so that every month you have a profit and loss account and balance sheet that reflect reality.
  • Recurring reporting. A periodic report tailored to your business, with the indicators you really need to follow and comparisons with the previous year or your budget.
  • Annual accounts. We prepare the balance sheet, profit and loss account and notes, and take care of filing them with the Companies Register.
  • Coordination with tax. Accounting is the basis for taxes. Because the same team handles both, what is declared always matches what has been booked.

An outsourced accountant for your SME

Many SMEs do not need, or cannot yet afford, their own accounting department. An outsourced accountant gives you the same result —up-to-date accounts, closes on time, reliable information— without having to hire, train and cover holidays or sick leave for an in-house employee. And if you already have someone in-house recording the day-to-day, we can take care of supervision, closes and annual accounts so that person has technical backing.

Which companies we work with

We work with limited and public limited companies in any sector: businesses that want to stop seeing their numbers once a year, startups reporting to investors, groups with several companies that need consistent figures, and family businesses looking to professionalise their financial information. Also with companies coming from another adviser who want more closeness and more control over what is done with their accounts.

How we work

The starting point is a review of your current accounting: how it is recorded, which criteria are applied, which balances need cleaning up and what information you need to receive. If you are coming from another adviser, we handle the data handover in an orderly way.

Then we set a calendar: what documentation you send us and when, the date by which each month is closed and when you receive your report. We work in Sage, although we adapt to the tools you already use; if you want to digitalise your financial control further, we explain how in this article.

Questions about Accounting

Do you only work with Sage?+

Sage is our main tool and where we are most efficient, but we adapt to how you operate and the integrations you already have.

How often will I get my accounts?+

We work with monthly closes, so you have up-to-date information throughout the year, not just at year-end.

My accounts are with another adviser, is it hard to switch?+

No. We take care of the data migration and handover in an orderly way, with no loss of continuity or information.

Do you prepare and file the annual accounts?+

Yes. We prepare the annual accounts, including the notes, and take care of filing them with the Companies Register.

Does it include tax filings?+

Accounting and tax go hand in hand. We can combine both services so everything is coordinated under a single point of contact.

What's the difference between an outsourced accountant and a bookkeeping firm?+

A bookkeeping firm usually focuses on recording and filing. As your outsourced accountant, we also close every month, send you useful reporting and help you interpret the numbers to make decisions.
